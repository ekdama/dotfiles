Yes. If your 0.655 mm H and 1.22 mm width are from a calculator for a 50 Ω microstrip, you should not automatically use 1.22 mm for CPWG. Adding ground beside the trace changes the impedance.

For your board, you need to choose three parameters:

H = 0.655 mm — fixed by your PCB stackup
W = RF trace width
G = gap from RF trace to top-layer GND
Practical CPWG starting point

If you want to keep the trace around the same size, I'd start by calculating CPWG with:

H = 0.655 mm
T = your finished copper thickness (~0.035 mm for 1 oz)
W = 1.0 mm
G = 0.20 mm
target = 50 Ω

Then adjust W and/or G based on the calculator's result.
